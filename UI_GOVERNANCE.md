# UI Governance

Gringotts keeps six primary destinations. v125 adds no primary destination and places close-trend explainability inside **Money → Close & Forecast**, with supporting views in existing Guided Plan and Reports surfaces.

## v125 presentation rules

- show the selected and comparison periods;
- label immutable close-snapshot evidence separately from open-month posted estimates;
- display confidence and its reasons near the headline;
- keep transfer-neutral and pending-exclusion boundaries visible;
- state that aggregate drivers are correlation, not proof of causation;
- place detailed close history behind progressive disclosure;
- preserve keyboard-accessible table regions and visible focus;
- avoid raw transaction, merchant, account, filename, or identifier detail in the trend surface.

## Direction after v125

v126 is a feature freeze focused on explicit lifecycle and action ownership. v127 reduces visible complexity and standardizes Preview, Save, Export, Restore, Delete, Reset, Cancel, confirmation, success, and failure language. Planning-only controls must never resemble execution controls.
